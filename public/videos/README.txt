=======================================================
GrainFlow Mandi - Login Background Video Guide
=======================================================

Apni video ko login page par chalane ke liye:

1. Apni video file ko copy karein (MP4 format behtareen hai).
2. Is folder me paste karein:
   d:\grainflow\public\videos\

3. Video ka naam rakhein:
   login-bg.mp4
   
   (Pura path banega: d:\grainflow\public\videos\login-bg.mp4)

System automatically is video ko login page ke right side par:
- Autoplay (خودکار پلے)
- Looping (مسلسل دہرائی)
- High Definition (فل سکرین HD)
me dikhaayega.

Agar aap chahein to video ko:
d:\grainflow\public\login-video.mp4
bhi rakh sakte hain, system dono paths ko check karta hai!
